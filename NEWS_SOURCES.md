# Sources — every story dated and checked

Entry: one page summing up **Wednesday 25 November 2026**. Rule applied to
every item below: the event happened, or was first reported, on Wednesday
25 November 2026 in some time zone, evidenced by the reporting itself
(a Nov 25 dateline, an "on Wednesday" in the copy, or a Nov 25 publication
timestamp) — never by the date of an index page that merely lists it.
Items whose event was 24 November or earlier were dropped, however big;
the largest are named at the end. Market figures are the Wednesday
25 November sessions: Asian closes of Nov 25 and European morning levels
of Nov 25. The US close of Tuesday 24 November is *not* used as
Nov 25 news anywhere on the page.
